# Couples conflict → game design brief (2026-09-29)

The owner asked for 2 high-octane games about couple relationships, grounded in basic psychology and the research behind *Fight Right: How Successful Couples Turn Conflict into Connection* (Drs. John and Julie Schwartz Gottman, 2024).

**Method:** web search in a cloud session. The egress proxy blocks most full-page fetches, so the facts below come from publisher pages and gottman.com search results, with URLs. **Our games use no book title, author name or trademarked term in player-facing text.** The concepts are general research findings, re-expressed as original mechanics.

## Psychology → mechanics
The core loop of each game *is* the finding, not a label stuck on it.

### Flood Line (`games/floodline`): "The Flood", plus repair and 5:1
| Finding | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Attack / defend / withdraw escalate the flood | The partner's barbs (4 shapes, one per horseman) fly at you. Each hit raises your heart rate; the water level on screen *is* your heart rate. |
| Antidotes | Tap a barb to answer it with its antidote. A caption names the skill (Soft reply, Appreciation, Take responsibility, Self-soothe). |
| Repair attempts, and masters accepting them | The partner also sends green repair attempts. Tapping (accepting) one lowers your heart rate and scores big; ignoring it counts as a negative. |
| Flooding at about 100 bpm: can't think or hear | Above 100 bpm you are flooded: taps fizzle and vision tunnels until you calm down. |
| Take a break and self-soothe | Hold to breathe: your heart rate falls fast and the fight slows (the break), but the clock keeps running. Knowing *when* to pause is the skill. |
| Start-up predicts the ending (96% in 3 min) | No hits in the first 8 s gives "Gentle start-up": ×2 points for the whole round. |
| 5:1 ratio | The end card shows positive:negative against the 5:1 benchmark. |

### Little Moments (`games/littlemoments`): bids for connection
| Finding | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Bids are small and constant | The partner sends a rapid stream of small round bubbles (a smile, "look!", coffee, a question, a song, a hug). Each lasts only a moment. |
| Turning toward | Tap a bid before it fades. Late catches count ("just in time"). |
| Turning away (distraction) | Phone notifications pop up as square look-alikes. Tapping one means you turned away: penalty and streak reset. Ignoring them is right. |
| 86% vs 33% | A live "turned toward" gauge with markers at 33% and 86%; the end card shows your %. |
| Emotional bank account | Bond meter: turning toward deposits, misses withdraw. The couple on the couch visibly lean in or drift apart; at zero they "drift apart" and the round ends. |
| Vulnerable bids matter most | Occasional big bids (double ring, "!") are worth more and fade faster. |

## Content rules
- All ages: no romance or sexual content, no insults in text (barbs are symbols, not words), no gore.
- Characters are two stylised, gender-neutral partners.
- Colour is never the only cue: shapes differ (round bids vs square phones; thorn, eye-roll, shield, brick, heart).
