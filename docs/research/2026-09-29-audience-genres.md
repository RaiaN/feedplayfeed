# Target audience and genre brief (2026-09-29)

Question from the owner: aim for high-octane, adrenaline, primal-instinct games ("a girl in the woods with wolves approaching", "a small fish in a dangerous pond"). Which genres fit best, and who is the target audience given Meta's Horizon Create/Studio direction?

**Method:** web search in a cloud session. The egress proxy blocked full-page fetches for most sources, so the claims below come from search-result snippets and summaries. Each claim keeps its URL. Figures from aggregator blogs are marked as such, and anything unverified says so.

## 1. Where the players are
- **Horizon Create/Studio** (waitlist opened Sept 24, 2026):
  - Prompt-to-game in 2D or 3D, distributed in Facebook, Instagram and Horizon feeds.
  - A player taps a clip in the feed and joins, including multiplayer, within seconds and with no download. https://runtimewire.com/article/meta-horizon-create-studio-ai-game-tools
  - Ranking: games that are "engaging, stable and culturally relevant, and that keep players returning" get shown to more people. https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26552/meta-reveals-ai-game-creation-tools-horizon-create-and-horizon-studio (search summary)
  - UNVERIFIED: no Connect 2026 statement on which age or gender Horizon games target.
- **Instagram:**
  - 18–24 is 31.7% and 25–34 is 30.6%, so about 62% of users are 18–34. The split is about 52% male / 48% female globally. https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-demographics-audience-creators-stats , https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-demographics/
  - Men 25–34 are the largest single segment, at about 16%. https://businesstats.com/age-distribution-of-worldwide-instagram-users/
- **Reels** (aggregator figures): about 62% of viewers are 18–34, with a near-even gender split. 18–24s watch about 44 min a day versus about 18 min for 35+. https://sendshort.ai/statistics/instagram-reels/
- **Facebook:** 25–34 is 31% and 18–24 is 22%. US use peaks at ages 30–49 (Pew 2025). https://www.demandsage.com/facebook-statistics/ , https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/
- **Reddit:** US 18–29 usage is 48%, and it leans male (29% of men vs 23% of women use it). Devvit favours daily and UGC games that "bring players back and spark discussion in the comments". https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/ , https://redditfunandgames.devpost.com/
- **Facebook Instant Games history:** short-burst casual, arcade, puzzle and sports games won. Basketball FRVR passed 4.2B plays, and EverWing averaged about 1 h a day per user. https://variety.com/2018/digital/news/facebook-instant-games-1202726455/ , https://www.pocketgamer.biz/blackstorm-labs-everwing-interview/

## 2. Evidence for primal-thrill themes
- **Horde survival (being hunted):** Survivor.io passed $500M in in-app purchases and was the top-grossing hybrid-casual game of 2023. It spent about 50% of its user-acquisition budget on native-looking gameplay clips. https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-43301 , https://naavik.co/digest/how-habby-mastered-tiktok-ua-for-survivor/
- **Grow or be eaten:** Hole.io (100M+ downloads) and the Hungry Shark / fish.io genre ("start small… avoid bigger fish"). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hole.io , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungry_Shark
- **Hide and chase:** Hide 'N Seek appears on lists of hyper-casual games with 250M+ downloads. https://nextbiggames.com/2025/11/14/top-hypercasual-games-with-over-250-million-downloads/
- **Near misses:** an "almost" result raises the motivation to keep going and activates reward circuits much like a win does. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near-miss_effect , https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10867214/
- **Ad clips:** fail or near-fail gameplay videos cut cost per install by about 55% versus success videos in one publisher's tests (https://www.mondayoff.me/MarketInsight/20/). Another reports satisfaction hooks beating fail hooks on click-through rate (https://www.playturbo.com/blog/creative-strategies-to-get-ua-right-for-hyper-casual-games), so test both.
- **Feed mechanics:** the first 1–3 s decide the scroll, and Meta favours a 3-second hook rate above 30%. About 85% of FB video plays muted. https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ad-creative-best-practices , https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/video-ads-guide
- **Retention benchmarks:** top hyper-casual D1 is 38–40% and D7 usually under 10% (about 15% for the best). https://segwise.ai/blog/mobile-gaming-app-user-retention-strategies . UNVERIFIED: no figures specific to predator/prey games.

## 3. Decision
- **Primary audience: 18–34 mobile short-video scrollers, male-leaning but near parity on Meta.**
  - They are the core of Reels time and the largest Instagram cohort, and they overlap with Reddit's young, male-leaning users.
  - Because women are near parity on Meta, danger stays stylised: tension, not gore.
- **Genres, in priority order:**
  1. **Predators closing in, survive until X:** horde pressure (Wolf Night).
  2. **Chase with a visible pursuer:** near-miss dodging (Shark Wake).
  3. **Freeze or flee from a hunter:** stealth tension with a sudden strike (Hawk Shadow).
  4. **Grow or be eaten:** small fish in a dangerous pond (next seed).
  5. **A daily seeded "survive the hunt" run with a leaderboard:** the Devvit wrapper for all of the above.
- **Rules for every game (from the evidence above):**
  - A threat is visible in frame 1 and a close call happens by second 2.
  - The game reads with the sound off.
  - Near misses are frequent and pay out.
  - Losses are fast, with instant retry.
  - A "seconds survived / close calls" stat people want to share.
  - Short, stable sessions, because ranking rewards engagement, stability and players returning.
