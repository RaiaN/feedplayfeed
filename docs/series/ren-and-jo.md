# Ren & Jo: series bible

One couple, many life situations; **each situation is its own game** (`games/renjo-<situation>`), built on `templates/duo-balance`. Research basis: `docs/research/2026-09-29-couples-conflict.md` (sources and verified facts).

## The couple
- **Ren** (blue `#56b4e9`, left of the seesaw): steady and a planner. Floods quietly: goes silent when overwhelmed.
- **Jo** (orange `#e69f00`, right): warm and spontaneous. Floods loudly: sighs and snaps when overwhelmed.
- Both are gender-neutral, stylised, and always the same pair. Their history carries across situations (the same faces, and later the same apartment, baby and parents).

## Core loop (every situation)
1. **The moment:** one partner speaks (a speech card with a timer).
2. **Answer for the other partner:** tap the left or right answer. Each answer moves **one** partner's meter. The chip on the button shows *who* it affects, not *which way*; knowing the psychology is the skill. After you answer, a label names the pattern (e.g. "Defensiveness ▼", "Asked about the dream ▲").
3. **Three card kinds**, mixed and seeded:
   - **Horseman vs antidote:** the same partner is affected, + or −. For example "You packed the kettle. Again?" → "My bad, I'll find it" (responsibility ▲) / "YOU labeled it!" (defensiveness ▼).
   - **Bid:** turn toward ▲ or away ▼.
   - **Dilemma (perpetual problem):** both answers are good, but for *different* partners ("Kitchen first" vs "Bed first"). This is the balancing act. Research: 69% of conflicts are perpetual differences, managed rather than solved.
4. **Pressure:** both meters drain as the situation wears on, the card timers shrink, and silence (timeout) stonewalls the speaker (−10).
5. **Big moment:** one spotlit climax card at ~62% of the round (slower timer, double points, ±~22).
6. **Worst case**, drawn as a scene with a closed door between them:
   - a partner's meter hits 0: **"{who} shut down"** (the book's *Flood* / *Chasm*)
   - or the seesaw stays tipped past ±45 for 2.5 s: **"one-sided"** (*The Standoff*: one partner keeps winning).
7. **Best case:** survive the situation and the warm scene plays (hearts, the couple side by side).
8. **Score:** points for positive answers × streak multiplier, a "just in time" bonus, a steady stream of points while balanced (±15), and a finish bonus. The share stat is **"in balance %"**.

## Content rules
- All ages: arguments shown through everyday words, with no insults beyond mild sarcasm, and no romance or sexual content. Affection is shown as hugs and hearts.
- No book titles, author names or trademarked terms in player-facing text. Research is cited in docs.
- Balance the two partners' lines: each partner is sometimes the harsh one and sometimes the hurt one.
